# Intent: add a Space from the title bar

Closing a Space from its tab takes one click, but turning it back on meant
opening Settings, finding the Space, turning on **Enabled** and choosing
**Save**. The owner approved a design for the way back: a plus after the
last Space tab.

The owner's requirements:

- A plus after the last Space tab, and at the end of the navigation rail
  when that layout is on, with the accessible name "Add a Space".
- Its menu, titled "Add a Space", lists every Space the product has that is
  switched off, each with its glyph, title and one-line description, and any
  preview mark the product already knows.
- Choosing a Space turns it on exactly as its **Enabled** switch and
  **Save** do, then switches to it. A notice confirms it, says a restart is
  needed, or says it could not be saved: the close path's three outcomes.
- With every Space on, the menu says so in one quiet line.
- **Manage Spaces…**, after a separator, opens Settings at the Spaces.
- The notice after closing a Space points to the plus: "Closed Actions. Add
  it again from the plus in the title bar."
- The plus stays reachable when the tabs overflow.
- A palette command, if the palette makes it small.
