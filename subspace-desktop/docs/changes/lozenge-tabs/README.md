# Glass capsule board tabs and title-bar spacing

The board tabs and the vertical rail's items are now capsules. The current
board's capsule is glass, in the macOS 26 Liquid Glass style. On macOS, the
title bar starts its content 20 pt after the traffic lights, and the lights,
logo, tabs and global actions share one centre line.

- [Intent](intent.md): what the owner asked for, including the squircle
  variant they considered and rejected.
- [Spec](spec.md): geometry, the glass material, theme tones, and which
  parts of Liquid Glass are reproduced.
- [Delivery](delivery.md): before and after screenshots in One Dark,
  Cupertino Light and Cupertino Dark, with horizontal and vertical
  navigation.
