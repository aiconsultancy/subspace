## About

Open **About** from the toolbar or the version in the status bar. The dialog
shows the product logo, name, version and build channel. The logo follows the
current light or dark theme. Development builds retain their full build version.

The update section shows the chosen GitHub account and **Check for updates**.
After an update is installed, use **Quit and restart** when ready. **No releases
published yet** means the channel has no releases, rather than a failed check.
The repository link opens GitHub. **Copy diagnostics** copies local support details.

Product credits appear unchanged in a quiet section at the bottom when the
product supplies them. **Close** or Escape returns to your Space.
